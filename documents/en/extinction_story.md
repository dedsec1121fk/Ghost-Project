# Extinction Story Archive

Extinction follows a separate Cryptid-focused storyline. Point of Contact begins the outbreak response; Nightfall reveals the secret research program around David Archer and Samantha Cross; Mayday continues the pursuit aboard the Stormbreaker and expands the Medusa/Cortex plot; Awakening takes the team into an Ancestor Ark beneath Ball's Pyramid; and Exodus concludes the main Ghosts Extinction arc around the Medusa Device, Cryptid Ancestors, and humanity's survival effort.
