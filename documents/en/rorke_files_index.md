# Rorke Files Index

There is one collectible in each of the 18 campaign missions. Full copyrighted recordings or transcripts are not bundled.

- **RF01 — Ghost Stories**: Introduces intelligence surrounding Gabriel Rorke's psychological history and earlier service.
- **RF02 — Brave New World**: Adds background on Rorke and the network tracking or studying him.
- **RF03 — No Man's Land**: Expands the archive around Rorke's military history and the search for the Ghosts.
- **RF04 — Struck Down**: Connects Rorke's campaign directly to Ghost casualties and captured personnel.
- **RF05 — Homecoming**: Adds operational context around the Federation offensive and Rorke's pursuit of the team.
- **RF06 — Legends Never Die**: Provides context for Rorke's history with Elias and the Ghosts before his defection.
- **RF07 — Federation Day**: Adds intelligence tied to Rorke's Federation contacts and Caracas operations.
- **RF08 — Birds of Prey**: Documents the hunt for Rorke as the Ghosts move to capture him.
- **RF09 — The Hunted**: Adds context around Rorke's rescue and the Federation's continuing pursuit.
- **RF10 — Clockwork**: Expands the strategic picture revealed by the stolen Federation data.
- **RF11 — Atlas Falls**: Connects Rorke's campaign to the Ghosts' attacks on Federation infrastructure.
- **RF12 — Into the Deep**: Provides additional operational context as the Ghosts strike naval assets.
- **RF13 — End of the Line**: Adds background related to the Federation weapons program and Rorke's role in the war.
- **RF14 — Sin City**: Deepens the archive surrounding Rorke immediately before and after the Las Vegas attack.
- **RF15 — All or Nothing**: Adds context to the final American counteroffensive and Rorke hunt.
- **RF16 — Severed Ties**: Links the ground offensive in Chile with the effort to take LOKI.
- **RF17 — Loki**: Adds information tied to the orbital battle and endgame against the Federation.
- **RF18 — The Ghost Killer**: Final campaign collectible, adding late-stage Rorke intelligence before the cliffhanger.
