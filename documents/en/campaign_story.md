# Campaign Story Archive

After a global energy crisis enables the Federation of the Americas to rise, the Federation turns the U.S. ODIN orbital-defense system against the United States. Ten years later, brothers Logan and Hesh Walker fight alongside the elite Ghosts under their father Elias. Their central enemy is Gabriel Rorke, a former Ghost commander who was abandoned during a disastrous Caracas operation, captured, and turned by the Federation. The Ghosts hunt Rorke while sabotaging Federation infrastructure and eventually discover LOKI, a Federation orbital kinetic-weapon platform. During the final American counteroffensive, U.S. forces seize LOKI and use it against Federation targets. Logan and Hesh attack Rorke aboard a train and believe they have killed him, but he survives and captures Logan, ending the campaign on a cliffhanger.

## Missions

### 01. Ghost Stories

**Date:** 2018-07-10 / 2028 framing  
**Locations:** ODIN space station, San Diego, California

The opening establishes the Ghost legend and shows the Federation hijacking the U.S. ODIN orbital weapon. ODIN is turned on American cities before the station is destroyed. Ten years later, Logan and Hesh Walker serve along the ruined U.S. frontier.

### 02. Brave New World

**Date:** 2028-06-06  
**Locations:** Liberty Wall, Fort Santa Monica, Los Angeles

Logan, Hesh and Riley return from the Liberty Wall after a Federation incursion. At Fort Santa Monica they receive new orders from Elias Walker, who is more closely connected to the Ghosts than his sons initially realize.

### 03. No Man's Land

**Date:** 2028-06-07  
**Locations:** No Man's Land, North of San Diego, California

Logan, Hesh and Riley cross the devastated zone beyond the Wall. They discover Federation activity around an ODIN crash site, learn that Gabriel Rorke is involved, and link up with Ghost operators Merrick and Keegan.

### 04. Struck Down

**Date:** 2028-06-07  
**Locations:** Federation-held stadium, San Diego area

The team assaults a Federation-held stadium to recover captured Ghost Alex 'Ajax' Johnson. Ajax is found mortally wounded, confirming that Rorke is systematically hunting the Ghosts.

### 05. Homecoming

**Date:** 2028-06-08  
**Locations:** Fort Santa Monica, Los Angeles, California

Federation forces launch a major offensive against Fort Santa Monica. Logan and Hesh help defend the position while the war closes in on the last major U.S. defensive line in the region.

### 06. Legends Never Die

**Date:** Flashback: 2016-07-08  
**Locations:** Caracas, Venezuela

A flashback shows the Ghosts under Gabriel Rorke during Operation Return to Sender. They kill Federation leader Diego Almagro, but catastrophic flooding forces Elias to leave Rorke behind to save the rest of the team. Rorke is later captured and turned by the Federation.

### 07. Federation Day

**Date:** 2028-06-15  
**Locations:** Caracas, Venezuela

The Ghosts infiltrate a Caracas skyscraper during Federation Day and interrogate Victor Ramos for information on Rorke's location. The mission exposes how deeply Rorke has become embedded in Federation operations.

### 08. Birds of Prey

**Date:** 2028-06-16  
**Locations:** Campeche oil fields, Gulf of Mexico

An Apache assault supports the Ghosts as they strike the oil fields where Rorke is hiding. The team captures him and begins transporting him back toward U.S. territory.

### 09. The Hunted

**Date:** 2028-06-17  
**Locations:** Yucatán jungle, Mexico

Federation forces destroy the Ghosts' transport aircraft and free Rorke. Logan is separated from the others in the Yucatán jungle and must survive pursuit before regrouping.

### 10. Clockwork

**Date:** 2028-06-20  
**Locations:** Andes Mountains, Argentine-Chilean border

Disguised in Federation uniforms, the Ghosts infiltrate a remote research base and steal computer data. The intelligence points toward larger Federation plans against the United States.

### 11. Atlas Falls

**Date:** 2028-06-25  
**Locations:** Atlas oil platform, Drake Passage / Antarctica

The Ghosts assault a key Federation oil platform to create a strategic distraction and weaken naval operations. The platform is destroyed as part of the broader plan to open a path toward Federation assets.

### 12. Into the Deep

**Date:** 2028-06-27  
**Locations:** Off the coast of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Logan and Keegan conduct an underwater operation against a Federation vessel equipped with a devastating sonar weapon. Logan uses a Proteus missile to destroy the ship.

### 13. End of the Line

**Date:** 2028-06-27  
**Locations:** Federation weapons factory, Rio de Janeiro region, Brazil

The Ghosts infiltrate a Federation weapons facility and discover that the Federation is developing its own orbital kinetic bombardment system, later identified as LOKI.

### 14. Sin City

**Date:** 2028-06-28  
**Locations:** Las Vegas, Nevada

Rorke captures the Ghosts at their Las Vegas safehouse. Elias Walker is executed in front of his sons, turning the conflict with Rorke into a deeply personal vendetta.

### 15. All or Nothing

**Date:** 2028-07-05  
**Locations:** USS Liberator, Pacific Ocean

The United States commits the USS Liberator in a final push to stop the Federation's orbital weapon. Logan and Hesh defend the carrier during a massive naval and air battle.

### 16. Severed Ties

**Date:** 2028-07-05  
**Locations:** Atacama Desert, Chile

U.S. ground forces assault the Federation's satellite-control infrastructure in Chile. Destroying the ground array makes it possible for an American space team to seize LOKI.

### 17. Loki

**Date:** 2028-07-05  
**Locations:** LOKI orbital station

A U.S. space team boards and commandeers the Federation's LOKI station. The captured orbital weapon is then turned against Federation military targets on the ground.

### 18. The Ghost Killer

**Date:** 2028-07-05  
**Locations:** Federation supply train, Atacama Desert, Chile, Pacific coast

Logan and Hesh board Rorke's train to avenge Elias. They apparently kill him after calling a LOKI strike on the train, but Rorke survives, ambushes the brothers on shore and drags Logan away, leaving the campaign unresolved.
